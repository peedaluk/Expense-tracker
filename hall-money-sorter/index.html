Start here

  1. Import your bank statement and credit card statement (CSV or Excel). Add as many files as you like.
  2. Transactions are sorted automatically as Hall, Personal or Transfer (for example paying your card bill). It recognises common shops and payment types, and learns from every choice you make.
  3. For the ones it can't tell, check its suggestions, or let 🤖 AI sort them (optional, see "Rules & backup"). Then quickly check what was auto-sorted.
  4. For a payment that was partly for the hall (a cash withdrawal, a mixed Amazon order), use Split.
  5. Add cash spending or cash income that isn't on any statement with "+ Add cash / other entry".
  6. Open Where the money stands to see how much hall money you're holding, or how much the hall owes you.

🔒 Everything stays in this browser on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Download a backup now and then from "Rules & backup".

🤖 AI sorting (optional)

The built-in smart sorting works offline and already handles common shops and patterns. For the hard ones (payments to people, unfamiliar shop names), Claude AI can read the descriptions and decide, using the choices you've already made as examples.

What gets sent to Anthropic (the company behind Claude): only the date, description, amount, money in or out, and bank or card, for the transactions being sorted. Long numbers like reference and card numbers are removed first. Your account number, balances and statement files are never sent. Descriptions can still contain names of people you paid.

Cost: you need your own API key from console.anthropic.com, and you pay Anthropic per use. Sorting a few hundred transactions usually costs well under a dollar. Your exact usage shows in the Anthropic console.
