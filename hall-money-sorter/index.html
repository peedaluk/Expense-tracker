Start here

  1. Import your bank statement and credit card statement (CSV or Excel). Add as many files as you like.
  2. Sort each transaction as Hall, Personal or Transfer (for example paying your card bill). Tick "remember" and similar transactions are sorted automatically.
  3. For a payment that was partly for the hall (a cash withdrawal, a mixed Amazon order), use Split.
  4. Add cash spending or cash income that isn't on any statement with "+ Add cash / other entry".
  5. Open Where the money stands to see how much hall money you're holding, or how much the hall owes you.

🔒 Everything stays in this browser on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Download a backup now and then from "Rules & backup".
